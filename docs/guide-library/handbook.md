# The Wave Guide Handbook

## Built For All Lifelong Learners And Leaders Building Waves For Humanity

**Core practice:** Trust People. And They Become Trustworthy.  
**Edition:** 1 — Practice, service, and shared work

A Wave is a custom home for something you are building, shaped with a real Guide, that people can help and pass along. A builder hires a human Wave Guide to listen, design, build, coordinate, and adapt that Wave as the mission changes.

The builder owns the direction. One lead Guide remains accountable for the relationship and can bring in supporting Guides with relevant experience. An external engagement is paid, with a defined build, weekly engagement, or monthly support agreed directly before work begins. Scope, fees, ownership, and ongoing care must be understood by both people.

The work follows a complete circle: understand the mission, define a next move, build a useful experience, coordinate contributions, show what changed, and adapt. The Wave retains its home and approved history as its needs change.

## Five Overlapping Practices

- **Design & Build:** Custom interfaces, tools, connections, and maintainable software.
- **Story & Community:** Clear storytelling, participation, consent, and people building together.
- **Growth & Distribution:** Useful invitations, channel-specific material, and people carrying the Wave.
- **Funding & Partnerships:** Clear resource needs, budgets, applications, evidence, and suitable collaborators.
- **Operations & Care:** Shared context, responsibilities, follow-through, risk review, and continuity.

These are areas of practice, not certifications. Guides describe what they can do, bring in suitable specialists, and record their actual role in the work.

## A Small Shared Foundation

Every Wave needs a recognizable identity and lasting home, a story, one primary next move, appropriate evidence, participation rules, an approved Journey, and a usable export. Its interface and tools can be entirely custom. The eight illustrated Wave types are starting models for discussion, not required templates or reported live outcomes.

Public stories and approved outcomes belong in the public Wave. Drafts, contact details, engagement terms, and client conversations belong in the assigned workspace. A contribution becomes public only with the required approval and consent.

## Use This Handbook

Work through the six lessons with a real brief or an explicitly illustrative exercise. Each lesson produces a deliverable and includes a readiness checklist. Use the seven worksheets as working documents, adapt them with the builder, and keep private records in the appropriate workspace.

This handbook teaches a working practice. It does not confer a credential, promise a role, guarantee funding, or replace professional advice or a suitable service agreement.
